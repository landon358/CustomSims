Work with Randy

Randy Norris has been competing at golf his whole life.

He played for St. Clair County Community College from 2009 to 2011, earned All-Region honors as a sophomore after finishing fourth at the NJCAA Region 12 championship, and went on to the NJCAA Division II National Championship in Scottsboro, Alabama, where he finished 70th in the country with a closing round of 73. He transferred to Cleary University and was named Athlete of the Year in 2013. In 2024 he was inducted into the SC4 Athletics Hall of Fame.

He still plays. He is a member at Willow Springs Country Club in Vassar, where he has been club champion ten times and runs the junior program. In the winter he runs a simulator league out of his own house.

That house league is where Custom Sims started. He built his own room, then built one for a friend, then for a club, and it turned into a business. Today Custom Sims LLC builds fully custom simulator rooms across Michigan and into northern Indiana, Illinois, and Ohio, for homeowners, golf clubs, and school programs.

Most people who install simulators learned golf second. Randy learned construction second. That is the whole difference, and you can see it in every room he builds.
