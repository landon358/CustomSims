Randy Norris · Owner & designer

Built by a golfer.

A lifelong golfer who learned construction second. Most simulator installers learned golf second. That is the whole difference.

Work with Randy

Randy Norris has been around golf his whole life.

He played college golf at St. Clair County Community College and Cleary University, and he still competes. He coaches junior golfers, and through the winter he runs a simulator league out of his own house.

That house league is where Custom Sims started — his own room first, then one for a friend, then one for a club. Today he builds fully custom simulator rooms across Michigan and into northern Indiana, Illinois, and Ohio, for homeowners, clubs, and school programs.

How he works

One crew, one trailer, one point of contact.

Every build is loaded out of a dedicated enclosed trailer and installed on site by the owner and crew. Framing, acoustics, lighting, turf, screen, calibration.

No such thing as guesswork. If your space will not work, you hear it on the first call instead of the last one.

Precision. Performance. Perfection.
