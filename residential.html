What a custom build includes

Framed and clad enclosure

Built on site and painted matte black inside, so no light bounces back into the room.

Pro-grade impact screen

Tensioned and framed to your room, sized so the image fills it corner to corner.

Tour-quality turf and mat

Hitting strip, landing zone, and floor turf that takes a real strike.

Launch monitor, mounted right

Selected for your ceiling and depth, then calibrated before we leave.

Projector, sized and aligned

No shadows, no keystone, no guessing at the throw distance.

Acoustic treatment

Panels where they matter, so a 170 mph ball speed does not shake the house.

LED lighting

Dimmable, color-adjustable if you want it, blacked out when you do not.

Software and cable management

Courses loaded, everything tucked away where you never have to look at it.
