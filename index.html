Why matte black

Details matter.

We paint every enclosure matte black for a reason. Any light that bleeds through the impact screen gets absorbed, not bounced around the room. More contrast. Cleaner image. Premium finish.

That is one decision out of a few hundred we make on every build. You will never see most of them. You will see the result.
